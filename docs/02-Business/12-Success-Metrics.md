# 1. Purpose

This document defines the measurable business outcomes used to evaluate the success of the FullScan Mobile Platform.

Success metrics provide objective indicators to determine whether the platform is achieving its intended business goals.

These metrics are reviewed periodically to drive continuous improvement.

# 4. Business Success Objectives

The platform is considered successful when it:

* Reduces fraudulent verification activities.
* Improves verification turnaround time.
* Increases operational efficiency.
* Improves evidence quality.
* Supports offline field operations.
* Provides trustworthy verification reports.
* Enables configurable business workflows.
* Reduces manual processing.

---

# 5. Success Metrics

## SM-001 — Verification Completion Rate

### Objective

Measure the percentage of assigned verification requests that are successfully completed.

### Target

≥ 95%

### Measurement

Completed Assignments ÷ Assigned Assignments × 100

---

## SM-002 — SLA Compliance

### Objective

Measure the percentage of verifications completed within the agreed service-level agreement (SLA).

### Target

≥ 95%

---

## SM-003 — Fraud Prevention

### Objective

Measure the reduction in fraudulent verification attempts.

### Indicators

* Mock location detections
* Blocked verification attempts
* Invalid device usage
* Unauthorized login attempts

### Target

Continuous reduction over time.

---

## SM-004 — Offline Success Rate

### Objective

Measure successful completion of verification activities without internet connectivity.

### Target

100% of supported offline workflows complete successfully.

---

## SM-005 — Evidence Quality

### Objective

Measure the percentage of verification submissions accepted without requiring rework.

### Target

≥ 98%

---

## SM-006 — Verification Accuracy

### Objective

Measure the percentage of verification reports accepted during quality review.

### Target

≥ 98%

---

## SM-007 — Assignment Productivity

### Objective

Measure the average number of completed assignments per Field Executive per day.

### Target

Defined by business operations based on regional requirements.

---

## SM-008 — User Adoption

### Objective

Measure active usage of the mobile application by assigned Field Executives.

### Target

100% of assigned Field Executives use the mobile application.

---

## SM-009 — Customer Satisfaction

### Objective

Measure employer satisfaction with verification quality and turnaround time.

### Target

≥ 90% positive feedback.

---

## SM-010 — Configuration Adoption

### Objective

Measure the percentage of business workflow changes implemented through configuration rather than application redevelopment.

### Target

≥ 80%

---

## SM-011 — Localization Effectiveness

### Objective

Ensure all supported languages provide an equivalent user experience.

### Target

100% localization coverage for supported languages.

---

## SM-012 — Platform Availability

### Objective

Measure the availability of the mobile platform for Field Executives.

### Target

Business operations are not interrupted by application defects or platform instability.

# 9. Continuous Improvement

Success metrics shall be reviewed periodically.

Where targets are not achieved, corrective actions may include:

* Business process improvements
* User training
* Workflow optimization
* Platform enhancements
* Additional configuration options
* Architectural improvements

# 12. Guiding Philosophy

> A successful platform is measured not only by technical excellence, but by the business value it delivers.

> Success is achieved when Field Executives complete trustworthy verifications efficiently, employers receive reliable evidence on time, and the platform adapts to evolving business needs through configuration rather than redevelopment.

Business success metrics provide the objective evidence that the FullScan Mobile Platform is fulfilling its intended purpose.
