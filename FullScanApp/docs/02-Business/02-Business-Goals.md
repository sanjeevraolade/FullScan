# Business Goals

**Document Version:** 1.0

---

# Overview

The FullScan Mobile Platform is intended to transform traditional field verification into a secure, configurable, and technology-driven digital process.

The platform enables organizations to perform reliable field verification while minimizing fraud, improving operational efficiency, and reducing dependency on manual processes.

The primary business goals focus on trust, transparency, configurability, scalability, and operational excellence.

---

# Primary Business Goals

## Goal 1 – Eliminate Fraudulent Field Verification

Ensure that every verification is performed only by an authenticated Field Executive at the assigned physical location.

The platform shall reduce opportunities for:

* Fake field visits
* Phone-only verification
* Gallery image uploads
* GPS spoofing
* Device sharing
* Unauthorized access

---

## Goal 2 – Improve Verification Quality

Provide employers with reliable and auditable verification evidence.

Each verification should include:

* GPS Coordinates
* Timestamp
* Device Information
* Geo-fenced Location Validation
* Live Camera Evidence
* Signature (where applicable)
* Verification Status
* Supporting Documents

---

## Goal 3 – Support Multiple Client Workflows

Different organizations require different verification processes.

The platform should support client-specific workflows without requiring application redevelopment.

Examples include:

* Address Verification
* Employment Verification
* Residence Verification
* Banking KYC
* Insurance Surveys
* Property Inspection

Business workflows should be configurable through server-provided metadata.

---

## Goal 4 – Enable Offline Operations

Field Executives often work in locations with limited or no network connectivity.

The application should allow users to:

* View assignments
* Complete verification
* Capture evidence
* Save progress
* Submit later

without requiring continuous internet connectivity.

---

## Goal 5 – Improve Operational Efficiency

Reduce manual effort by digitizing the complete field verification lifecycle.

The platform should minimize:

* Paper forms
* Manual data entry
* Duplicate work
* Repeated visits
* Delayed submissions

---

## Goal 6 – Increase Business Agility

Business teams should be able to modify verification workflows through configuration instead of application releases.

Configuration should support:

* Dynamic Forms
* Dynamic Sections
* Dynamic Validation
* Dynamic Attachments
* Dynamic Workflows
* Dynamic Master Data

---

## Goal 7 – Build a Reusable Verification Platform

The platform should become reusable across multiple industries and verification domains.

Future use cases include:

* Police Verification
* Asset Inspection
* Telecom Verification
* Banking KYC
* Insurance Surveys
* Healthcare Home Visits
* Government Field Surveys

The same mobile application should support these use cases through configuration.

---

## Goal 8 – Ensure Enterprise Security

The application shall enforce enterprise-grade security through:

* Device Registration
* Single Device Login
* Biometric Authentication
* Mock Location Detection
* Root/Jailbreak Detection
* Secure Storage
* HTTPS Communication

Security validations are mandatory and cannot be bypassed.

---

## Goal 9 – Deliver Consistent User Experience

Provide a consistent user experience across Android and iOS while supporting:

* Light Theme
* Dark Theme
* System Theme
* English
* Hindi
* Telugu

Future languages and themes should be supported with minimal changes.

---

## Goal 10 – Build for Long-Term Extensibility

The architecture should support future capabilities including:

* OCR
* AI-assisted Verification
* Face Matching
* Document Validation
* Feature Flags
* Plugin-based Widgets
* Dynamic Business Rules
* Remote Configuration

The platform should evolve through extensibility rather than architectural redesign.

---

# Business Goal Summary

The FullScan Mobile Platform aims to become a secure, configurable, offline-first, and enterprise-grade field verification platform that enables organizations to perform trustworthy verification while adapting rapidly to changing business requirements through configuration rather than application redevelopment.
